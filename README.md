# Alessandro Saglia

## EN

I build and study digital systems where software, governance, and regulation intersect.
With a dual background in law and computer engineering, I am particularly interested in how systems behave under real operational and regulatory constraints, especially in public-sector and healthcare contexts.

### Core Focus & Interests

- **Public Digital Infrastructure**: designing for interoperability, resilience, and operational sustainability in complex institutions.
- **AI Accountability & Governance**: translating legal frameworks and institutional constraints into technical specifications and operational processes.
- **Resilient & Local-First Systems**: homelab infrastructure, privacy by design, compliance by design, and architectures that minimize unnecessary data exposure.

### Currently working on

- 📦 **`cup-check`**: developing a local-first validator and Python library for Italian public project codes (CUP).
- 📝 **`insight`**: maintaining structured notes and long-form writing on digital governance, agentic AI accountability, post-quantum transition, and sovereign infrastructure.
- 🤝 **Open Source**: contributing to open-source tooling, localization, and developer infrastructure projects.
