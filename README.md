# Alessandro Saglia

## EN

I build and study digital systems where software, governance, and regulation intersect.

Interested in:
- compliance by design
- privacy by design
- public digital infrastructure
- AI accountability
- interoperability in complex institutions
- local-first architectures
- governance of socio-technical systems

I am particularly interested in how systems behave under real operational and regulatory constraints — especially in public-sector and healthcare contexts.

Most repositories here are tools, experiments, notes, or infrastructure related to those themes.
