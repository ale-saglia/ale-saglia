# Alessandro Saglia

## EN

I build and study digital systems where software, governance, and regulation intersect. With a dual background in law and computer science, I am particularly interested in how systems behave under real operational and regulatory constraints — especially in public-sector and healthcare contexts.

**Core Focus & Interests:**
- **Public Digital Infrastructure:** Designing for scale, interoperability, and resilience in complex institutions.
- **Accountable AI & Governance:** Translating legal frameworks and operational constraints into technical specifications.
- **Sovereign Systems:** Homelab infrastructure, local-first architectures, privacy by design, and compliance by design.

**Currently working on:**
- 📦 **`cup-check`**: Developing a local-first validator and Python library for Italian public project codes (CUP), pushing towards the 0.5.0 release.
- 📝 **`insight`**: Maintaining structured research notes on digital governance, agentic AI accountability, and sovereign infrastructure.
- 🤝 **Open Source**: Actively contributing to engineering platforms and tools (e.g., adding Italian localization to `kilocode`).
